# Research Report: Second-Order Economic and Geopolitical Effects of Commercial Deep-Sea Mining in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone

## TL;DR
- **Negative Fiscal Asymmetry for Producing States:** Empirical trade data and macroeconomic modeling indicate that commercial deep-sea mining (DSM) in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone (CCZ) creates net fiscal losses for terrestrial mining states: projected government mining tax contractions of **$1.1–$1.19 billion/year** at peak production exceed total International Seabed Authority (ISA) royalty collections of **$0.55–$1.1 billion/year**, leaving the UNCLOS Economic Assistance Fund structurally underfunded ([DSCC / Wilde et al. Summary](https://deep-sea-conservation.org/deep-sea-mining-could-destroy-economic-value-and-cost-governments-more-than-it-promises-to-deliver-new-analysis-finds); [Planet Tracker](https://planet-tracker.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Race-to-the-Bottom.pdf)).
- **Acute Concentration Risk in Vulnerable Economies:** Twelve developing and resource-dependent nations rely on copper, cobalt, nickel, and manganese for **5% to 37.5% of GDP** and **$560 billion/year** in combined export earnings, led by the **DRC** (73% of global cobalt, 79.0% metal export share), **Indonesia** (67% of global nickel, $460M/yr projected tax loss), **Zambia** (71.4% export share, 37.5% of GDP), and **Gabon** (world #2 manganese producer, $110M/yr projected tax loss) ([USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf); [Planet Tracker](https://planet-tracker.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Mining-for-Trouble.pdf)).
- **2025–2026 Governance Fragmentation:** Multilateral governance under the ISA has diverged sharply from unilateral national permitting. While **46 countries** support a moratorium or precautionary pause ([ISA FAQ](https://isa.org.jm/faq-for-media)) and the ISA pushed its Mining Code roadmap into 2027 ([RIFS Potsdam](https://www.rifs-potsdam.de/en/blog/2026/08/how-govern-deep-ocean)), the April 2025 U.S. Executive Order fast-tracked unilateral permitting under DSHMRA—enabling The Metals Company (TMC) USA to file a consolidated NOAA recovery application covering **~65,000 km²** in the CCZ ([ISA Statement](https://isa.org.jm/news/statement-on-the-us-executive-order-unleashing-americas-offshore-critical-minerals-and-resources); [Markets Insider](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/tmc-usa-files-first-consolidated-deep-seabed-mining-application-increasing-expected-commercial-recovery-permit-area-to-65-000-km2-1035736361)).
- **Project Economics vs. Market Reality:** Contractor pre-feasibility assumptions (e.g., NORI-D's **$4.9B CAPEX** targeting **12 Mtpa** wet nodules by Q4 2027) rely on bullish metal price decks ($56,000/t cobalt, $20,295/t nickel) that exceed independent "likely" market scenarios ($36,000/t cobalt, $17,600/t nickel) and overlook potential 20% offtaker/blending discounts ([Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/projects/nori-d-polymetallic-nodule-project-us); [EJF Reassessment](https://ejfoundation.org/resources/downloads/Summary-briefing-A-Financial-Viability-Reassessment-of-Deep-Sea-Mining-under-Realistic-Cost-and-Price-Assumptions.pdf)).
- **Tool Design Implication:** Your interactive risk assessment tool should explicitly separate **verified empirical baselines** (USGS production, CEPII BACI export dependency, DISCOL/APEI ecological baselines) from **scenario-based projections**, pairing commodity price triggers with fiscal buffer calculators and regulatory-pathway timelines.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Primer: Marine Geology & International Maritime Law for Policymakers
- **What is the Clarion-Clipperton Zone (CCZ)?** The CCZ is a **6,400-km-wide** abyssal plain in the northeast equatorial Pacific Ocean between Hawaii and Mexico, lying at water depths of **4,000 to 6,000 meters** ([SEC NORI Technical Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1798562/000121390021029413/fs42021a1ex96-1_sustainable.htm); [ABC30 / Sweetman](https://abc30.com/post/scientists-discover-dark-oxygen-being-produced-more-13000/15084853)). Its seafloor hosts **polymetallic nodules**—potato-sized mineral concretions formed over millions of years containing **manganese (~31%), nickel (~1.4%), copper (~1.1%), and cobalt (~0.13%)**—estimated at **>20 billion tonnes** across the zone, including ~270 million tonnes of nickel ([Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/projects/nori-d-polymetallic-nodule-project-us); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/09/20/deep-sea-mining-the-race-for-critical-minerals-used-in-clean-energy.html)).
- **Who Governs "The Area"?** Under the 1982 **United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)**, the seabed beyond national 200-nautical-mile Exclusive Economic Zones (known as *the Area*, covering ~54% of global oceans) is designated the **"common heritage of humankind."** The **International Seabed Authority (ISA)**, headquartered in Kingston, Jamaica, regulates both mineral exploration (31 active exploration contracts) and environmental protection in the Area ([ISA Statement](https://isa.org.jm/news/statement-on-the-us-executive-order-unleashing-americas-offshore-critical-minerals-and-resources); [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/photos-ecological-havoc-mining-rare-metals-ocean-floor-2023-7)).
- **Why Unilateralism Is Possible:** The United States never ratified UNCLOS. Instead, U.S. domestic law—the **Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act of 1980 (DSHMRA)**—allows the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) to issue licenses to U.S. entities in international waters, creating a direct legal clash with UNCLOS Parties ([Harvard EELP](https://eelp.law.harvard.edu/tracker/deep-sea-mining); [Cambridge / Fisher & Robb 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/who-governs-deepsea-mining/3D3195EE9CF48AB616A53755EA9BEAF8)).

### 3. Terrestrial Mining Economies: Vulnerability & Risk-Scoring Matrix
Because polymetallic nodules co-produce four metals simultaneously (nickel, cobalt, copper, manganese), a single commercial operation injects supply into markets with vastly different elasticities. **Cobalt** and **manganese** face acute price sensitivity due to smaller global market sizes (310 kt/yr and 20 Mt/yr respectively in 2025), whereas **copper** (23 Mt/yr) absorbs seabed supply more easily ([USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf)).

### 4. Governance Fragmentation & Geopolitical Scenarios (5-, 10-, and 15-Year Horizons)

Multilateral rulemaking at the ISA has fractured along three axes: **(1) Environmental Precaution** (46 states backing a moratorium/pause, including the UK, France, Germany, Brazil, Canada, Chile, and Peru, plus corporate buyers like BMW, Volvo, and Samsung) ([ISA FAQ](https://isa.org.jm/faq-for-media); [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/photos-ecological-havoc-mining-rare-metals-ocean-floor-2023-7)); **(2) Strategic Resource Competition** (China becoming the ISA's largest funding contributor in 2026 while holding multiple exploration contracts, prompting U.S. countermeasures) ([CRS R47324](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47324)); and **(3) Unilateral Legal Bypassing** (the April 24, 2025 U.S. Executive Order directing NOAA/BOEM to expedite DSHMRA permits in international waters, leading TMC USA to expand its U.S. application from ~25,000 km² to **~65,000 km²**) ([Harvard EELP](https://eelp.law.harvard.edu/tracker/deep-sea-mining); [Markets Insider](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/tmc-usa-files-first-consolidated-deep-seabed-mining-application-increasing-expected-commercial-recovery-permit-area-to-65-000-km2-1035736361)).

### 5. Quantifiable Environmental Externalities & Threshold Gaps
To evaluate ecological stabilization alongside economic disruption, policymakers need empirical metrics from CCZ field trials rather than theoretical assurances:

- **Multi-Decade Benthic Recovery Lag:** Twenty-six years after the 1989 **DISCOL** disturbance experiment in the Peru Basin nodule field, **suspension-feeder fauna remained significantly reduced** in disturbed tracks, proving abyssal ecosystem recovery spans multiple decades to centuries ([SEC NORI Technical Report / Simon-Lledó et al. 2019](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1798562/000121390021029413/fs42021a1ex96-1_sustainable.htm)).
- **Scale Multiplier (Trials vs. Commercial):** The spring 2021 **Patania II** pre-prototype collector test (monitored by MIT and MiningImpact 2) directly impacted **≤0.1 km²** of CCZ seafloor, whereas a single full-scale commercial operation will strip **several hundred km² per year**—a >1,000× spatial scaling jump ([Frontiers in Marine Science 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2022.882155/full); [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2022/sediment-deep-sea-mining-0921)).
- **Protected Area Connectivity Deficits:** The ISA's 2012 CCZ Environmental Management Plan designated **9 Areas of Particular Environmental Interest (APEIs)**, each **400 km × 400 km** plus buffer zones, protecting **>30% of the CCZ management area** ([Ocean Mining Intel](https://oceanminingintel.com/news/regulation/the-5-minute-briefing-the-clarion-clipperton-zone)). However, a 2025 biogeographic review found **APEI3 shared ≤2 isopod species** with nearby mining contract areas, demonstrating that protected zones may not effectively replenish mined habitats ([Frontiers in Marine Science 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2025.1547803/full)).
- **Electrochemical "Dark Oxygen" Risk:** A 2024 *Nature Geoscience* study led by Andrew Sweetman detected **abiotic molecular oxygen production** at ~4,000 m depth near CCZ polymetallic nodules in total darkness, suggesting nodule removal could eliminate a previously unknown benthic oxygenation mechanism ([CNBC / Sweetman 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/07/23/dark-oxygen-discovered-in-the-deep-sea-in-groundbreaking-study.html)).

### 6. Actionable Policy Mechanisms & Trigger Thresholds
Under **UNCLOS Article 151(10)** and the 1994 Part XI Implementation Agreement, the ISA must establish an **Economic Assistance Fund** using contractor payments to assist developing land-based producer states suffering *"serious adverse effects on their export earnings or economies"* ([UNCLOS Part XI Agreement](https://www.un.org/depts/los/convention_agreements/texts/agreement_part_xi/agreement_part_xi.htm)). However, empirical modeling shows administrative costs and Economic Assistance Fund deductions (**$22–$93M/yr**, averaging **$80M/yr**) leave nowhere near enough revenue to offset **$1.1–$1.19B/yr** in terrestrial tax losses ([Planet Tracker](https://planet-tracker.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Race-to-the-Bottom.pdf); [DSCC](https://deep-sea-conservation.org/deep-sea-mining-could-destroy-economic-value-and-cost-governments-more-than-it-promises-to-deliver-new-analysis-finds)).

#### Recommended Trigger Thresholds & Policy Responses for the Risk Assessment Tool
1. **Commodity Price Shock Trigger:**
   - *Threshold:* Sustained (>2 quarters) global price drop below **$16,000/t Nickel** (forcing high-cost mines into care-and-maintenance, as seen in Australia's 54% drop in 2025) or **<$30,000/t Cobalt** paired with DSM commercial ramp-up (>5 Mtpa).
   - *Policy Action:* Activate sovereign stabilization buffers (e.g., Indonesia's *Danantara* model or DRC's dynamic quota/royalty adjustments) and trigger ISA Council review of production ceilings/royalty tiers.
2. **Fiscal Contraction Trigger (Developing Producer States):**
   - *Threshold:* Projected annual mining tax loss exceeding **5% of national mineral tax receipts** or **>0.5% of GDP** in countries with >25% metal export dependency (DRC, Zambia, New Caledonia, Chile, Gabon).
   - *Policy Action:* Pre-negotiate bilateral/multilateral **Concessional Transition Financing** and direct ISA Economic Assistance Fund disbursement formulas *before* exploitation contracts are granted.
3. **Governance & Trade Integrity Trigger:**
   - *Threshold:* Issuance of unilateral commercial recovery permits outside ISA jurisdiction (e.g., NOAA/DSHMRA approval of NORI-D).
   - *Policy Action:* UNCLOS Parties and EU/G7 OEMs enforce **ESG Chain-of-Custody & Port-State Import Bans** on non-ISA seabed minerals ("untouchable metals" doctrine), denying market access and project insurance.
4. **Ecological Threshold Trigger (ISA Standards & Guidelines):**
   - *Threshold:* Sediment plume dispersion exceeding contract boundary buffers, or failure to verify species connectivity (>50% taxonomic overlap) between mined blocks and adjacent APEIs.
   - *Policy Action:* Automatic **suspension of collector operations** under the ISA's Regional Environmental Management Plan (REMP) framework (`ISBA/30/C/L.3/Rev.1`) ([ISA 30th Session](https://isa.org.jm/news/international-seabed-authority-holds-a-landmark-30th-session-the-assembly-adopts-historic-decisions-as-negotiations-on-the-mining-code-progress)).
