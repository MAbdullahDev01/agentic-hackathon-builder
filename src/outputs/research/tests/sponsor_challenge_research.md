# Research Report: Lab-to-Market IP Translation in Solid-State Battery Electrolytes

## TL;DR
- **Data Infrastructure Alert (2026):** Legacy `patentsview.org` APIs and search entered migration to the **USPTO Open Data Portal (ODP)** starting **March 20, 2026** ([USPTO Transition Guide](https://data.uspto.gov/support/transition-guide/patentsview)). To avoid broken pipelines, anchor your patent-to-paper graph on the **Lens.org API (`2.19.3`)**, which natively indexes **150M+ patents and 300M+ scholarly works** with pre-resolved Non-Patent Literature (`cites_resolved_npl`) and inventor ORCIDs, paired with **Semantic Scholar's Academic Graph (S2AG)** for citation velocity and `SPECTER2` embeddings ([Lens API Docs](https://docs.api.lens.org); [Semantic Scholar API](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api)).
- **CPC `H01M 10/0562` Is Too Broad Alone:** Official USPTO CPC definitions assign `H01M 10/0562` to *all* inorganic solid electrolyte materials (sulfides, oxides, and halides combined) ([USPTO CPC H01M](https://www.uspto.gov/web/patents/classification/cpc/html/cpc-H01M.html)). Your pipeline must intersect `H01M 10/0562` (and primary-cell halogenide codes `H01M 6/182`–`6/183`) with stoichiometry/chemistry filters (`halide`, `oxyhalide`, `Li3YCl6`, `Li3InCl6`, `Li2ZrCl6`, `LiTaOCl4`).
- **Concentrated IP vs. Rapid Academic Diffusion:** Globally, **>860 patent records across >330 families** were filed on halide solid electrolytes through late 2023, led by **Panasonic/Sanyo, Samsung, Saint-Gobain, GLABAT, SVOLT, and TDK**, with **>60 new applicants entering since 2022 (80% from China)** ([KnowMade](https://www.knowmade.com/technology-news/press-release/a-new-comprehensive-patent-landscape-report-on-halide-solid-electrolytes-for-li-ion-batteries)). Key academic patent holders include **Western University, USTC, University of Maryland, University of Waterloo (with BASF), Yonsei University, and Hanyang University** ([KnowMade / Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/halide-solid-electrolytes-li-ion-133400783.html)).
- **Why 18 Months Is the Right White-Space Cutoff:** Patent applications remain unpublished for **18 months** after their earliest priority date, while most global jurisdictions (EPO, CNIPA, JPO, KIPO) enforce **absolute novelty** ([Thompson Patent Law](https://thompsonpatentlaw.com/when-to-get-a-patent); [Blue Over Gray](https://www.blueovergray.com/patent-filing-grace-period)). Flagging "unprotected white space" only **>18 months post-publication** prevents false positives caused by pending unpublished filings.
- **Entity Resolution Must Survive Name Collisions & Migrations:** Common researcher names (e.g., *Cheng Ma*, *Linda Nazar*) collide across unrelated industries, and graduate/postdoctoral inventors routinely shift from university affiliations to corporate assignees. Combining ORCID matching, co-author/co-inventor graph overlap, and the **Reliance on Science** confidence framework (`1–10` scale, threshold `≥ 4`) solves traceability ([Reliance on Science](https://relianceonscience.org/patent-to-paper-citations)).

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Halide Solid-State Electrolyte Breakthroughs & Leading Academic Labs (2022–2026)

Halide solid-state electrolytes (HSSEs) bridge the performance gap between sulfides (high conductivity, poor oxidation/moisture stability) and oxides (high stability, poor sinterability/processability). Between 2022 and 2026, academic breakthroughs shifted from first-generation rare-earth chlorides (`Li3YCl6`, `Li3InCl6`) toward cost-effective Zr-based halides (`Li2ZrCl6`), superionic oxyhalides (`Li–M–O–Cl`), and ultra-high-voltage fluorides.

| Material Class | Representative Formulas | Room-Temp Ionic Conductivity | Key Technical Advantage / Breakthrough | Leading Research Groups & Commercialization Posture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tri-halide Chlorides** | `Li3InCl6`, `Li3YCl6`, `Li3LaCl6` | `1–4 mS/cm` (`Li3InCl6`) | Dry-air processability; direct 4 V oxide cathode compatibility without coatings ([Xnergy](https://xnergy.us/li3incl6-halide-solid-electrolyte)) | **LBNL** (Guoying Chen; redox-active `Li3YCl6` Tech ID `2026-027`, patent pending for licensing, [LBNL IPO](https://ipo.lbl.gov/2026/02/09/redox-active-halide-solid-electrolytes-for-stable-high-energy-all-solid-state-batteries-2026-027)); **Univ. of Waterloo** (Linda Nazar; BASF collaboration/IP, [KnowMade](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/halide-solid-electrolytes-li-ion-133400783.html)) |

| **European Basic-Science Leaders** | Halide–sulfide interfaces (`Li6PS5Cl`), atmospheric-pressure ASSBs | Interface characterization & low-pressure cycling | Quantifies halide–sulfide chemical incompatibility (*Adv. Giessen / Münster** (Jürgen Janek, Wolfgang Zeier), **Collège de France** (J.-M. Tarascon) — high-citation academic groups less prominent among top halide patent assignees |

**Product Implication for Non-Expert Summaries:** Investors and R&D directors do not read `Li3YCl6` vs. `LiCl-4Li2TiF6` stoichiometry tables intuitively. Your pipeline should classify each lab along two commercial axes: (1) **Cell Role** (bulk *catholyte* for 4–5 V cathodes vs. *separator* vs. *interfacial coating*) and (2) **Commercialization Posture** (*Active TTO Licensing*, *Corporate Co-Assignment*, *Spinout/Industry Consortium*, or *Open-Science Basic Research*).

### 2. Global Patent Landscape, Classification Taxonomy & IP Claim Categorization

#### Market & Filing Concentration
According to KnowMade's global halide patent landscape analysis ([KnowMade Press Release](https://www.knowmade.com/technology-news/press-release/a-new-comprehensive-patent-landscape-report-on-halide-solid-electrolytes-for-li-ion-batteries); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/halide-solid-electrolytes-li-ion-133400783.html)):
- **Volume:** **860+ patents and applications** across **330+ patent families** from **110+ entities** (through September 2023), accelerating sharply post-2022.
- **New Entrants:** **60+ organizations** filed their very first halide electrolyte patent in **2022 or later**, with **80% of new entrants originating from China** (e.g., EVE Energy, Liongo New Energy, Qingtao Energy, BYD, SVOLT, GLABAT).
- **Corporate vs. Academic Leaders:** **Panasonic/Sanyo** holds the dominant corporate portfolio, challenged by **Samsung, Saint-Gobain, GLABAT, SVOLT, TDK, Toyota, and EVE Energy**. Leading academic/institute assignees include **University of Western Ontario, USTC, University of Maryland, BASF/University of Waterloo, KETI, Hanyang University, Yonsei University, Ningbo University, University of Dayton, and University of Liverpool**.

#### Automated IP Claim Categorization (Three-Bucket Classifier)
Following industry patent taxonomy ([KnowMade](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/halide-solid-electrolytes-li-ion-133400783.html); [SciOpen](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.14062/j.issn.0454-5648.20250405)), parse independent claim text (`claims.claim_text` in Lens) into three functional buckets:
1. **Bulk Material Composition:** Claims reciting stoichiometric formulas (`Li_a M_b X_c`), doping/substitution (`Zr`, `Hf`, `Ta`, `Nb`, `Y`, `In`, `O`-substituted oxyhalides), crystal phases, or halide–halide/halide–sulfide mixtures.
2. **Electrode–Electrolyte Interface Engineering:** Claims reciting *core-shell* structures, protective cathode coatings (`LNMO`, `NCM`), artificial solid-electrolyte interphases (SEIs) against lithium-metal anodes, or composite catholyte architectures.
3. **Scalable Manufacturing & Synthesis:** Claims reciting *mechanochemical ball-milling*, *co-melting*, *ammonium-assisted wet chemistry*, or *water/solvent-based synthesis* (e.g., THF, acetonitrile, deionized water) and drying parameters.

### 4. Publication-to-Patent Lag, Legal Rules & Commercialization Signals

#### Why Patent Timing Precedes or Follows Publication
1. **Patent-First Strategy (Negative Paper-to-Priority Lag):** Commercializing labs (e.g., Western University, Waterloo/BASF, UMD, Yonsei) routinely file a provisional or domestic priority application **before** submitting a manuscript to *Science*, *Nature Energy*, or *ACS Energy Letters*. Because EPO, CNIPA, JPO, and KIPO enforce **absolute novelty**—where any prior public paper or preprint destroys foreign patentability—filing first preserves global rights ([Blue Over Gray](https://www.blueovergray.com/patent-filing-grace-period); [Mewburn Ellis](https://www.mewburn.com/law-practice-library/grace-periods-for-disclosure-of-an-invention-before-applying-for-a-patent)).
2. **The 12-Month Grace Period Trap:** Only the U.S. (35 U.S.C. § 102(b)(1)) and a few jurisdictions allow filing up to **12 months** after an inventor's publication. Crucially, under **USPTO MPEP 2153.01(a)**, if an academic paper lists **more co-authors than inventors** named on the later patent application, the USPTO can treat the paper as disqualifying prior art unless the applicant proves the extra co-authors did not contribute the claimed invention ([Northeastern CRI](https://cri.northeastern.edu/grace-periods-not-always-so-graceful)).
3. **Why Some High-Impact Labs File Zero Patents:** Groups focused on computational screening, fundamental crystallography, or failure characterization (rather than scalable synthesis or novel compositions) either intentionally publish into the public domain to accelerate pre-competitive science, lack university TTO budgets for PCT national-stage filings, or publish preprints before filing—permanently forfeiting global patent rights.

#### Defining Rigorous "Unprotected White Space" & Commercialization Indicators
- **18-Month Secrecy Buffer:** Because patent applications are not published until **18 months** after their earliest priority date ([Thompson Patent Law](https://thompsonpatentlaw.com/when-to-get-a-patent)), a 2025/2026 paper with zero visible patents may already be protected by an unpublished filing. Your pipeline should compute:
  $$\text{Lag}_{\text{days}} = \text{Date}_{\text{earliest\_priority}} - \text{Date}_{\text{paper\_pub}}$$
  and only flag **"Unprotected White Space"** when:
  1. $\text{Today} - \text{Date}_{\text{paper\_pub}} > 18\text{ months}$ (548 days),
  2. No patent family matches via `cites_resolved_npl` or inventor-author overlap, **and**
  3. Academic impact exceeds a benchmark threshold (e.g., top 10% `citationCount` in S2AG for halide SSEs).
- **Commercialization & Spinout Early-Warning Signals:**
  - **Applicant-Added vs. Examiner-Added NPL Citations:** In Lens (`reference_cited.npl.cited_phase`), `APP` (applicant-submitted) self-citations indicate intentional IP-to-paper linkage, whereas `EXA` / `SEA` citations with category `X` or `I` indicate an examiner using the lab's paper to *reject* a competitor's patent claims ([Lens Patent Request](https://docs.api.lens.org/request-patent.html)).
  - **Continuation Family Clustering:** Multiple active continuation filings sharing an identical `earliest_priority_date` (e.g., `US18/959,854` → `US19/082,675`, `US19/221,851`, `US19/299,584` all anchored to `2023-11-28`, citing halide papers like Jung et al., *ACS Energy Lett. 2022*, [Google Patents US12365598B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12365598B2/fr)) signal aggressive commercial claim-broadening around a core platform.
  - **Assignee & TTO Shifts:** Co-assignment with industry (e.g., BASF/Waterloo), active TTO licensing disclosures (`patent pending` status such as LBNL `2026-027`), or an inventor's transition from a `University` affiliation on 2022 S2AG papers to a `Startup`/`Corporate` assignee on 2024–2025 patent filings.

### 5. Entity Resolution & Confidence Scoring Blueprint

Naive string matching across academic and patent databases fails severely in materials science due to common researcher names (e.g., searches for *"Cheng Ma"* surface unrelated profiles at Meta and Crédit Agricole alongside USTC's battery leader; *"Linda Nazar"* collides across unrelated industries) and Romanization/name-order variations.

| Resolution Tier | Matching Signals | Recommended Weight / Score | Failure Mode Mitigated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Deterministic IDs** | Exact **ORCID** match (`S2AG author.externalIds.ORCID` ↔ `Lens biblio.parties.inventors.orcid`); exact **DOI** in `reference_cited.npl` | **0.95 – 1.00** (Verified) | Completely eliminates homonym collisions when ORCID is populated (supported in Lens Patent API since Sept 2024, [Lens Changelog](https://docs.api.lens.org/support.html)) |
| **Tier 2: Graph + Affiliation** | Exact/normalized name + `≥ 1` shared co-author/co-inventor **OR** matching standardized institution (`University of Western Ontario` ↔ `Western University`) | **0.75 – 0.94** (High Confidence) | Handles institutional name aliases and missing ORCIDs on older patents |
| **Tier 3: Migration / Spinout Match** | Name match + `≥ 1` shared co-inventor/co-author + `SPECTER2`/CPC topical similarity, but **assignee shifts** from University (`t`) to Startup/Corp (`t + 1..4 yrs`) | **0.60 – 0.74** (Flag: `AFFILIATION_SHIFT`) | Captures PhD/postdoc talent moving to industry or launching spinouts—a prime commercial signal |
| **Tier 4: Ambiguous / Collision** | Name string match only, **zero** co-author overlap, and mismatched topic/city | **< 0.40** (Discard / Flag `COLLISION_RISK`) | Prevents false attribution on high-frequency names (analogous to Reliance on Science's `confidence ≥ 4` cutoff out of 10, [Reliance on Science](https://relianceonscience.org/patent-to-paper-citations)) |

**Actionable UI Output for R&D Directors:** Present each researcher/lab in a relational matrix displaying: **Clickable Standardized Identifiers** (`https://doi.org/{doi}` and `https://www.lens.org/lens/patent/{lens_id}` or verified patent application/publication number), **Entity Confidence Badge** (`0.00–1.00` with `AFFILIATION_SHIFT` / `COLLISION_RISK` flags), **Paper-to-Priority Lag (days)**, **IP Claim Scope** (*Composition / Interface / Synthesis*), and a plain-English **Commercialization Posture Summary**.
